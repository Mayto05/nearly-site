# Nearly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Nearly is a contraction timer for iPhone and Apple Watch made by Anastasiia Shalankina. The short version: nothing leaves your devices.

## What Nearly collects

Nothing. Nearly has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own. The app makes no network requests of its own; purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store.

## What Nearly stores on your devices

- The contractions you time: start and end of each one.
- Your settings: the rule you chose (5-1-1, 4-1-1, 3-1-1 or your own), the phone numbers you saved for the call button, notification switches.
- Whether Nearly Lifetime has been purchased.

This data is stored in the app's private container on your iPhone and Apple Watch and is synced between the two directly. It is included in your device backups under Apple's own privacy terms, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

## Sharing

The log leaves your phone only when you share it yourself — as a PDF or text through the system share sheet. Nearly sends nothing to anyone on its own.

## Phone numbers and calls

The numbers you save for the call button stay on your device. Tapping the button starts a regular phone call through your iPhone or Apple Watch.

## Third parties

Nearly uses no third-party SDKs, shares no data with anyone and sells no data. There are no ads.

## Children

Nearly does not collect data from anyone, including children.
