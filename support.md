# Nearly — Support

Nearly is a contraction timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. Questions and problems: shalankinkv@gmail.com. You will get a reply from the people who made the app.

## Common questions

**How do I time a contraction?** Tap the big button when it starts and tap again when it ends — in the app, on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island or on Apple Watch.

**What do Early, Closer, Call and Go mean?** Nearly compares your contractions with the rule you chose in Settings (5-1-1 by default: five minutes apart, one minute long, for one hour). The rule is a guideline — agree on it with your care provider.

**I tapped by mistake / forgot to tap.** Use Undo right after a wrong tap. In History you can add a contraction you missed, edit one or delete it.

**The Lock Screen timer does not appear.** The Live Activity is part of Nearly Lifetime and needs Live Activities to be allowed for Nearly in iPhone Settings.

**How do I restore my purchase?** Settings in Nearly > Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

**Where is my data?** Only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no account and no cloud. Deleting the app deletes the log.

Nearly is not a medical device. In an emergency call your local emergency number.

## Still stuck?

Write to shalankinkv@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.
