# Nearly – Contraction Timer

A contraction timer for iPhone and Apple Watch that works without unlocking your phone. Tap when a contraction starts, tap when it ends; Nearly tells you in plain words when to call and when to go.

Nearly is not a medical device. Decide when to leave together with your care provider.
